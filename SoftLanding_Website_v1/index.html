Questions

The honest answers.

01

Why not just install an AI plugin?

A plugin is a tool. It has no persistent account, no authority to cancel, and no one accountable if something goes wrong. We sign a contract, carry insurance, and a human reviews the action.

02

Do you store my bank password?

No. Email is read-only OAuth. Bank data is statements or official access only. We never ask for a password.

03

What can my family see?

Only what the account owner and the family member agree to. Parent seats see subscriptions, charges and scam alerts—not passwords. Teen seats see categories and risk alerts, with the teen informed.

04

Is my data used to train AI?

No. Local AI processes receipts; we do not train on your data. You can revoke access and request deletion at any time.

05

What happens if I die?

The digital will is optional. You choose share, seal or delete; a trusted contact and verification trigger the release. It is never automatic.

06

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly and annual plans can be cancelled from the monthly brief. Cancellation stops the next charge; unused time is not refunded unless required by law.
