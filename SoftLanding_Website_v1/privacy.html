Privacy / 隐私

Privacy, in plain language.

This is a pilot site for SoftLanding. It does not set cookies, run analytics, or load tracking pixels. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message; we use them only to reply and to decide whether to invite you to the pilot.

What this site does not do

  • It does not ask for a bank password, an ID number, or a payment card.
  • It does not use your email content to train a model.
  • It does not sell or rent personal information.

What happens when the product launches

Product access is read-only and permissioned. Email access uses OAuth; bank data is limited to statements or official access. Local AI processes receipt metadata on the device. You can revoke access and request deletion at any time. Every deletion gets a receipt.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or a privacy concern: hello@softlanding.example. We reply within five working days.

中文

隐私，说人话。

这是 SoftLanding 的内测网站。本站不设置 Cookie、不接分析工具、不加载追踪像素。如果你发邮件给我们，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容，只用于回复你、判断是否邀请你参加内测。

本站不会做的事

  • 不索要银行密码、身份证号或银行卡号。
  • 不用你的邮件内容训练模型。
  • 不出售或出租个人信息。

产品上线后

产品访问是只读、可授权的。邮箱使用官方 OAuth 授权；银行只使用账单或官方授权；本地 AI 在设备内处理收据元数据。你可以随时撤回授权、要求删除；每次删除都有回执。

联系

问题、删除请求或隐私顾虑：hello@softlanding.example。我们会在五个工作日内回复。
