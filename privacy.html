Privacy隐私

Privacy, in plain language. 隐私说明，尽量说人话。

Last updated: November 2026最后更新：2026 年 11 月

This page explains what happens to the information you send us. It is written to be read, not to hide behind. 这一页说明：你发给我们的信息，会去哪里、被谁看到、保存多久。我们尽量写得能看懂，而不是藏在条款里。

What we collect我们收集什么

  • The name, contact details, role and message you enter in the contact form.你在联系表单里填写的称呼、联系方式、身份和留言。
  • Basic technical information from our hosting provider (such as IP address, browser type and time of visit) for security and reliability.托管服务商提供的基础技术信息（如 IP 地址、浏览器类型、访问时间），用于安全和稳定。
  • We do not ask you to send ID documents, death certificates or financial account details through this form.我们不会要求你通过这个表单发送身份证件、死亡证明或金融账户信息。

Why we collect it为什么收集

To reply to you, to understand what you need, to provide the service if you become a client, and to keep a record of the case. We do not sell your information. 为了回复你、了解你的需要、在你成为客户后提供服务，以及保留案件记录。我们不出售你的信息。

Who can see it谁可以看到

  • The SoftLanding case team, on a need-to-know basis.SoftLanding 的案件团队，按“需要知道”原则访问。
  • Netlify, our hosting and form-processing provider, which stores the submission and sends us a notification.Netlify，我们的托管与表单处理服务商；它保存提交内容并向我们发送通知。
  • Professional partners (such as a lawyer or notary) only with your consent and only for your case.专业伙伴（如律师、公证员）仅在征得你同意、且仅为你的案件时才会接触。

Where it is processed在哪里处理

Our form and hosting are provided by Netlify, whose infrastructure may be outside mainland China. By submitting the form, you understand this. If you prefer a local process, write to us first and we will arrange it. 我们的表单与托管由 Netlify 提供，其基础设施可能位于中国大陆以外。提交表单即表示你理解这一点。如果你希望走本地流程，请先写信给我们，我们会另行安排。

How long we keep it保存多久

Up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you become a client or the law requires a longer period. You can ask us to delete it sooner. 自最后一次联系起最多保存 12 个月，除非你成为客户或法律要求更长期限。你可以要求我们提前删除。

Your rights你的权利

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent. Write to care@softlanding.co and we will respond within a reasonable time. 你可以要求查看、更正或删除我们持有的你的信息，也可以撤回同意。写信到 care@softlanding.co，我们会在合理时间内回复。

Cookies and analyticsCookie 与分析

This site does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. If that changes, this page will be updated first. 本站目前不使用广告 Cookie 或第三方分析工具。如果以后使用，我们会先更新这一页。

Contact联系

Questions about this page: care@softlanding.co. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. 关于这一页的问题：care@softlanding.co。这是通俗说明，不构成法律意见。
