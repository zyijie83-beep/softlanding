No one is trained
for the paperwork. 没有人教过我们，
如何料理身后事。

A life leaves behind more than memories: certificates, banks, insurers, pensions, subscriptions, accounts, cloud photographs. Families do this while grieving — often from another city, usually without a map. 一个人走后，留下的不只是回忆：证明、银行、保险、养老金、订阅、账号、云端的照片。家人要在悲痛里处理这些事，常常还在另一座城市，而且没有一张地图。

The funeral is a ceremony. The months after are a project — with deadlines, institutions, and no project manager. 葬礼是一场仪式。之后的几个月，却是一个项目——有截止日期，有无数机构，却没有项目经理。

Miss a deadline and money is lost. Miss an account and it renews for years. Miss a document and the family starts again. The work is invisible until it is overdue. 错过一个期限，钱就没了。漏掉一个账户，它可能自动续费好几年。少一份文件，全家就得从头再来。这些事平时看不见，直到逾期才浮现。
